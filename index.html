FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does property management cost? +

Pricing depends on property type, unit count, and service scope. We provide a clear proposal after an initial consultation.

How often are owner reports provided? +

Every month — a clear statement covering rent collected, expenses, and any maintenance activity, with photos when relevant.

Can I manage my property remotely if I live abroad? +

Yes. Diaspora owners are a core part of who we serve — you'll get regular updates and one dedicated contact, wherever you are.

How are maintenance requests handled? +

Tenants report issues to us directly, we dispatch a vetted contractor, and document the work with before-and-after photos.

Do you work with newly completed developments? +

Yes — we partner with developers to manage buildings after handover, so residents get a responsive management team from day one.
