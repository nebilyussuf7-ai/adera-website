Frequently Asked Questions

How much does property management cost? +

Pricing depends on property type, unit count, service scope, and reporting needs. Adera provides a clear proposal after consultation.

How often are owner reports provided? +

Owners receive regular financial and operational reporting, with dashboard visibility for income, expenses, vacancies, and maintenance updates.

How does the AI platform work? +

The platform supports faster communication, smarter maintenance routing, automated summaries, and clearer owner reporting while the Adera team remains in control.

Can I monitor my property remotely? +

Yes. Owners can monitor key property information remotely through digital reports and dashboard-style visibility.

How are maintenance requests handled? +

Tenants submit requests, Adera coordinates response, tracks progress, and keeps owners informed about cost, urgency, and resolution.
